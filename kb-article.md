# My login stopped working and I can’t get into my computer

## Symptom

“My login just stopped working. I typed my password and it keeps rejecting me, so I can’t get to the banking system, and there are people waiting in line.”

## Cause

The account was disabled in Microsoft 365, which blocks sign-in even when the password is right. Accounts can be turned off for security reasons, such as a policy requirement or an account that needs review, which keeps bank systems protected from unauthorized access.

## Resolution steps

1. Read the message on the screen and make a note of what it says.
2. Check that your keyboard isn’t set to all caps, then try your password one time.
3. If it’s still rejected, stop typing it.
4. Open a ticket with the IT service desk under **Teller workstation issue**. Tell them it’s a login problem and that customers are waiting.
5. Include the message you wrote down, and have your name and branch ready so IT can confirm it’s you.
6. Wait for IT to reply that your account is back on, then sign in again.
7. If you still can’t get in after that, restart your computer and try once more, then reply on the same ticket.

## Watch out for

Trying your password again and again won’t help and could lock you out too. Don’t borrow a coworker’s login to get around it, because that puts their access at risk. One careful try with the keyboard check is all you need before you ask for help, so don’t wait to report it if customers are waiting.
